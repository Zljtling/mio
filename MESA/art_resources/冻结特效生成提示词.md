Use case: stylized-concept. Create a square opaque PIXEL ART ice texture for a game freeze effect, based on reference image: overlapping angular aqua cyan ice plates with pale white branching cracks. This is a texture blended into the silhouette of an80x80 player sprite, NOT an illustration of a frozen character and NOT a UI icon. Cover the ENTIRE canvas from edge to edge with pale turquoise glacier ice, medium teal facets, irregular fractured polygon plates of varied sizes, delicate but legible bright frost-white/cyan jagged cracks crossing in several branching directions. Flat retro low-resolution game pixel art designed on an80x80 grid, enlarged with crisp square pixels; no antialiasing, no smooth blur, no painterly strokes. Palette only light ice cyan, muted aqua, medium turquoise, pale blue, white. Broad ice planes interspersed with6to10 major crack paths so it stays clear when reduced to80x80. Some tiny pixel chips along fissures, restrained contrast to avoid hiding player form after blending. Similar geometric layered fractured texture to the reference. No black outline or dark frame around canvas. No text, no symbols, no character, no egg, no objects, no surrounding background, no transparent gaps, no shadows, no border. Fully opaque square texture, no checkerboard. Prefer1280x1280 output with16px blocks to represent80x80 pixels.
